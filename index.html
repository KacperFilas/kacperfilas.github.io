// What I do

Art and tech, both sides

I build tools and pipelines, fix rendering issues and keep visuals within the performance budget.

  1. 01

    Tools & automation

    Editor tools in Unity and Blender, C# and Python scripts: asset validation, naming conventions, automated import, materials and prefabs from templates. AI pipelines in ComfyUI.

  2. 02

    Shaders & optimization

    Shaders in HLSL and Shader Graph, materials and assets built to performance budgets, scene profiling.

  3. 03

    Level & environment art

    Scenes from blockout to final lighting, terrain from GIS data and Gaea, level design.

  4. 04

    VFX & animation

    Effects in Particle System, VFX Graph and Niagara, animation with Animator and Timeline.
